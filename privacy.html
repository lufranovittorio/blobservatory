Privacy policy: Blobservatory uploader

Last updated: 10 November 2026

"Blobservatory uploader" is a private tool used only by the owner of the Blobservatory YouTube channel (@blobservatoryy) to manage that channel. It is not offered to other users.

What it accesses

  • Through YouTube API Services, it uploads and schedules videos to the owner's channel, posts and reads comments on the channel's own videos, and reads the channel's public statistics and YouTube Analytics reports.
  • It does not access, collect or store personal data of any other Google or YouTube user. Public comments on the channel's videos are read only to understand viewer feedback, and are not sold or shared.

Storage and sharing

  • OAuth tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to third parties.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google and YouTube terms

This tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
