Blobservatory

Blobservatory is a YouTube Shorts channel of short animated "What happens if…" science explainers starring Wob, a clay-style blob test subject.

Channel: youtube.com/@blobservatoryy

"Blobservatory uploader" is the channel's own internal tool: it uploads and schedules the channel's videos, posts the channel's own comments and reads the channel's statistics through the YouTube API. It is not offered to other users.
